## Generated data

The questionnaire corpus (`benchmark/docs-rfq.jsonl`), its query sets, and the
fine-tuning pairs in `benchmark/gap2_pairs.jsonl` are output from
`openai/gpt-4o-mini` via [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai), generated by
`benchmark/build_synthetic_rfq_corpus.py` and `benchmark/gap2_gen_pairs.py`. The
vendors, products, control taxonomy and question set are fictional; see
[`benchmark/README.md`](benchmark/README.md). No third-party questionnaire,
vendor document or scraped corpus is included in this repository.
